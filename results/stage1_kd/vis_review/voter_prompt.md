# How well can you see the knife in the green box?

The sheets come from a lab camera looking down at one person: dark shirt and trousers, wooden floor, office chairs, an orange cart.
Each sheet has 12 cells. Each cell is a zoomed crop with a green box and a yellow id number at the top left.
The green box is where a person labelled a knife.

The knife is a small kitchen knife with a dark handle and a dark or silver blade, about one hand long.

For every id, answer one letter:

- `c`: **clear.** Looking at this crop, you can tell there is a knife: the blade, or the handle clearly sticking out of the hand, has a knife shape. It may be small or dark, but it is sharp enough to recognise.
- `b`: **blurred by motion.** The hand or arm is moving fast, and the knife is a smear or streak whose shape you cannot make out.
- `h`: **hidden or barely visible.** The knife is inside the fist, behind the body or the hand, lost against dark clothes, or too small to see. You would not know there is a knife without being told.
- `x`: **no knife** at the green box, not even a hidden one: wrong place, an empty hand, or another object.

If you hesitate between `c` and `h`: answer `c` only if a stranger looking at this crop would say "that person holds a knife".

Look at each sheet image yourself with the Read tool. Judge every cell on its own; cells are not related to each other.
