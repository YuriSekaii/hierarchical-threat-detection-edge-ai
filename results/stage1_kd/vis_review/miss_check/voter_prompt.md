# Is the knife label right?

The sheets come from a lab camera looking down at one person: dark shirt and trousers, wooden floor, office chairs, an orange cart.
Each sheet has 12 cells. Each cell is a zoomed crop with a yellow id number at the top left.

- The **green box** is where a person labelled a knife. This is what you judge.
- A **thin yellow box with a number** is a detector's guess. Ignore it when you answer; it is only there for context.

The knife is a small kitchen knife with a dark handle and a dark or silver blade, about one hand long.

For every id, answer one letter about the green box:

- `c`: **correct.** There is a knife you can clearly recognise (blade, or the handle clearly sticking out of the hand, has a knife shape), and the green box sits on it: it covers the knife, maybe with some hand.
- `w`: **wrong box.** A knife is clearly visible in the crop, but the green box does not sit on it: it misses most of the knife, or is on another spot.
- `b`: **blurred by motion.** The hand or arm moves fast and the knife is a smear whose shape you cannot make out.
- `h`: **hidden or barely visible.** The knife is inside the fist, behind the body or hand, lost against dark clothes, or too small. You would not know there is a knife without being told.
- `x`: **no knife** at the green box, not even a hidden one: an empty hand, or another object.

If you hesitate between `c` and `h`: answer `c` only if a stranger looking at this crop would say "that person holds a knife".

Look at each sheet image yourself with the Read tool. Judge every cell on its own; cells are not related to each other.
