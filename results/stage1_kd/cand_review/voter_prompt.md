# Knife check: what is inside the red box?

The images are sheets from a lab camera looking down at one person: dark shirt and trousers, wooden floor, office chairs,
an orange cart. Each sheet has 12 cells. Each cell is a zoomed crop with a red box and a yellow id number at the top left.

For every id on your sheets, answer one letter about what is inside the red box:

- `a`: a knife (blade and/or handle) is inside the red box and the box roughly fits it.
- `k`: a knife is there, but the red box clearly does not fit it. The box covers only a small part of the knife, is far off, or is many times bigger than the knife.
- `n`: no knife inside the red box. Examples: an empty hand, a fist, fingers, clothing, a chair part, the cart, a phone, wallet, pen or remote.
- `u`: cannot tell. Too blurred, too dark, or too small.

What the knife looks like: a small kitchen knife with a dark handle and a silver or dark blade, about the length of a hand.
- It is often held pointing down along dark trousers, where it is hard to see. Look along the fingers and below the fist for a thin dark or shiny line.
- In a fast swing it is a grey or dark smear past the hand.

Do not answer `a` only because a hand is in the box. Do not answer `n` only because the area is dark. If you cannot decide, answer `u`.

Look at each sheet image yourself with the Read tool. Judge every cell on its own; cells are not related to each other.
